Drishti

An observability pipeline in Go (OTLP, Kafka, ClickHouse) that turns telemetry into alerts in seconds, and a LangGraph agent that finds which service caused the alert and proposes a fix for a human to approve.

This page replays real investigations the agent ran on a recording it had never been tuned on. They were picked by a rule fixed in advance, and the wrong and unsure ones are here on purpose.
